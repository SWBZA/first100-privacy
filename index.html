Privacy Policy — The First 100

Last updated: 19 September 2026
Applies to: The First 100 (Android)
Published by: Quorum Digital Solutions (Pty) Ltd, South Africa

The short version

The First 100 collects nothing. It transmits nothing. It has no server.

Everything you write stays in the app's private storage on your own device. We cannot read it, because there is no mechanism by which it could reach us.

What we collect

Nothing.

The app has no account system, no sign-up, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party software development kits that collect data. It makes no network requests to any server operated by us or by anyone else.

What the app stores, and where

All of it stays on your device, in storage private to the app:

  • Your name, role, and start date
  • Journal entries, including any audio recordings you choose to retain
  • Stakeholder records and your notes about them
  • Personnel and talent assessments
  • Early wins, risks, resources, and process records
  • Weekly checkouts and cycle reviews
  • Your settings

No copy of any of this is held anywhere else.

Dictation

Dictation is transcribed on your device, using Android's on-device speech recognition. Your voice is not uploaded to us or to any third party.

Where a device cannot perform recognition locally, the dictation feature is not offered at all, rather than falling back to a service that would send your audio elsewhere.

Microphone access is requested only when you tap Dictate, and audio is captured only while you are recording. Audio is retained only if you explicitly switch that setting on, and it is then stored on the device alongside the entry.

Permissions, and why

Permission Why
RECORD_AUDIO Dictation. Used only while you are actively recording.
INTERNET Required by the application framework. The app makes no network requests.
Notifications Local reminders scheduled on your device. No push service is involved.

Your data is not encrypted

Stated plainly, because it matters and because most applications are vague about it: the data stored by this app is not encrypted on your device.

The Personnel Assessment section can be protected by a PIN or your device biometrics. That controls access to the interface. It does not encrypt the underlying records, and someone with technical access to an unlocked device could read them.

The app says this in-product too, in the section it applies to. Protect your device, and use its lock screen and full-device encryption.

Your control over your data

  • Export. You can export everything at any time as a plain JSON file, from Settings. That file is yours. It is unencrypted, so store it somewhere safe.
  • Import. You can restore from a previously exported file.
  • Delete. Clearing app data, or uninstalling, permanently deletes everything. There is no backup held elsewhere and no way for us to recover it.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for you to request from us, and nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Children

The app is intended for working professionals and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not have any data to share.

If you purchased the app through Google Play, your purchase and payment are handled by Google under its privacy policy. We receive only the aggregate sales information Google provides to developers. We never receive your payment details.

Data protection law

Because we neither collect nor process your personal information, we are not a responsible party in respect of it under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), nor a controller or processor under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Please note that if you record information about other people in the app — colleagues in your stakeholder map or personnel assessments, for example — you are the party responsible for that information under applicable law. It resides on your device, under your control, and we have no access to it or ability to act on it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address and the date above will change. Any change that materially affects how the application handles your information will also be described in the app's release notes.
